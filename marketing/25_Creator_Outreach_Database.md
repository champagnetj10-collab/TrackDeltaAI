## Data-quality notes (read before using this)

**On contact emails.** YouTube deliberately CAPTCHA-gates the business email on channel
About pages — that's an anti-harvesting measure and we don't route around it. Every email
in this file is either (a) published openly on the creator's own website, or (b) marked
`NOT VERIFIED`. Where no public email exists, the realistic first contact is a DM or their
own contact form. Don't guess at addresses; a bounced cold email hurts domain reputation.

**On audience numbers.** Figures below are from the creator's own site or a third-party
directory (Feedspot, Jul 2026). Treat as approximate. Verify before citing a number back
to a creator — getting someone's own subscriber count wrong in an outreach email is worse
than not mentioning it.

**On personalization.** Every "Why them" line below traces to something actually published
by that creator. If a cell says `NEEDS RESEARCH`, the email is NOT ready to send — go watch
two or three of their recent videos first and rewrite the hook. Do not send a templated
message with a name swapped in; that is the exact thing this campaign is trying not to be.

## Competitive landscape context (for anyone doing outreach)

Be aware before you talk to anyone in this space — these already exist and creators will
likely bring them up:

- **Trophi.ai** — real-time voice coaching, 4 sims, founded by Scott Mansell (Driver61).
  1.5M+ coaching sessions claimed. The most direct competitor.
- **RaceData AI** — telemetry tool, per-corner phase breakdown (braking/entry/apex/exit).
- **VRS** — reference laps + telemetry, now mostly a hardware company.
- **Garage 61** — free telemetry comparison, popular with league racers.

**Do not pretend TrackDelta is the first AI coaching tool.** Creators in this niche know
better, and claiming it would destroy credibility instantly. The honest differentiator is
the *Driver DNA* angle — modelling why an individual drives the way they do across many
sessions, rather than diffing them against one reference lap.
